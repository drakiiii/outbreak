Outbreak — epidemic simulator

A browser port of this project's age-structured, stochastic SEIR engine. Set the disease, then layer on vaccination, lockdowns, ICU capacity and seasonality and watch the epidemic curve respond. Transmission is calibrated from the next-generation matrix to hit your target R₀; every run reports the effective reproduction number Rₜ, hospital and ICU burden, attack rate and deaths.
